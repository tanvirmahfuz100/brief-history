*Use this section to understand the examiner's priorities before generating new questions.*

**Exam Structure Overview:**
- **Duration:** 1 hour (Mid-Term) / 3 hours (Final)
- **Format:** "Answer any four" (Final) / "Answer all" (Mid-Term)

**High-Frequency Topics (Guaranteed Appearances):**
| Topic | Exam Frequency | Core Skill Required |

ii). Materials in the amount of $22000 were issued from the storeroom for use in production.
iii). Requisitions for indirect materials and supplies amount to $2000/
iv). The total payroll for the month was $35000 including salesman’s salaries of $5000 and office salaries of $5000. Labour time tickets show that $20000 of the payroll was direct labours. v). The employer’s payroll taxes consist of $1440 FICA, 1.4% State unemployment insurance, and 0.5% Federal unemployment insurance tax.

ix). Customers were billed for $122000 for the shipments made during May.
x). Customers paid $68000 through bank account.

### Part 3: Guidelines for AI Question Generation

*Instructions for the AI: When asked to generate new practice questions for "Cost Accounting (AIS 3101) - University of Barisal", adhere strictly to the following rules to match the examiner's style.*

#### 1. Template for "Overhead Variance Table" Questions
**Pattern:** Provide a 7-row table (A to G) with 5 columns. Leave 2-3 cells blank per row.
**Data Rules:**
- **Spending Variance** = Actual FOH - Budget Allowance.
- **Idle Capacity Variance** = Budget Allowance - Applied FOH.
- *Trick Row:* Include one row where Spending Variance = 0 (Actual = Budget). Include one row where Idle Capacity Variance = 0 (Budget = Applied). Include one row where variances are **Favorable** (shown in parentheses).
- **Currency:** Use **BDT** (Bangladeshi Taka) or **Tk.**.
- **Numbers:** Keep values in the range of 15,000 to 40,000 for realism.

#### 2. Template for "EOQ and Inventory Levels" Questions
**Pattern:** Provide a scenario with weekly/monthly demand, ordering cost, carrying cost, and **Lead Time Range** (e.g., 4 to 6 weeks) and **Usage Range** (Max, Min, Normal).
**Required Calculations:** (i) EOQ (ii) Re-order Level (iii) Minimum Level (iv) Maximum Level (v) Average Stock Level.
**Key Formula to Test:**
- **Re-order Level = Max Usage × Max Lead Time** (This is the most critical test point).

#### 3. Template for "Labor Cost & Incentive" Questions
**Pattern:** Provide a table of daily output (Mon-Fri), an hourly rate, and a standard output per hour.
**Required Plan:**
- **Part (i):** Halsey Premium Plan (50% sharing of time saved) or a variant (e.g., 90% sharing as seen in Q6.1).
- **Part (ii):** 100% Bonus Plan or Piece Rate with Guaranteed Wage.
**Structure:** Always require a schedule showing **Conversion Cost per Unit**.

#### 4. Template for "Spoilage/Rework" Journal Entries
**Pattern:** Provide cost per unit (Materials, Labor, OH %). Specify a defect rate (e.g., 120 units defective out of 1000). Provide cost to correct defects.
**Required Scenarios:**
1.  **Charge to Specific Job:** *Debit WIP, Credit Materials/Payroll/Applied OH.*
2.  **Charge to Factory Overhead:** *Debit Factory Overhead Control, Credit Materials/Payroll/Applied OH.*

#### 5. Journal Entry Nuances (Critical for Authenticity)
When generating a "Cost Flow Journal Entry" question (Q1 style), you **MUST** include the following complex payroll calculations to match the University of Barisal standard:
- Gross Payroll vs. Net Payroll (withholding taxes).
- Separation of Direct Labor vs. Indirect Labor vs. Sales Salaries.
- **Employer's Payroll Taxes** (FICA, State/Federal Unemployment) charged to Factory Overhead.
- **Vacation Pay Accrual** charged as a % of payroll to Factory Overhead.

#### 6. Language & Currency Convention
- **Currency:** Always use **BDT** or **Tk.** followed by numbers (e.g., BDT 39,300).
- **Dates:** Use recent years (2022, 2023, 2024, 2025).
- **Names:** Use letters for companies (e.g., S Company, D Inc., M Ltd.).

### Part 4: Example of AI-Generated Question (For Reference)
*This demonstrates the correct application of the guidelines above.*

**Sample Generated Question:**
**(Q. Variance Analysis)** R. Manufacturing operates at a normal capacity of 60,000 machine hours. Fixed overhead is estimated at BDT 24,000 and variable overhead at BDT 36,000. During March 2024, actual hours were 55,000 and actual overhead was BDT 58,000.
*(a)* Compute over/under applied overhead.
*(b)* Complete the missing figures for Company Z Inc.:

| Set | Actual FOH | Applied FOH | Budget Allowance | Spending Variance | Idle Capacity Variance |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| A | 45,000 | 48,000 | 50,000 | ? | ? |
| B | ? | 22,000 | 28,000 | (1,000) | ? |
| C | 30,000 | ? | 30,000 | 2,000 | (2,000) |

**Solution Key (For AI self-check):**
- *Set A Spending:* 45k - 50k = (5,000) Fav. *Idle Capacity:* 50k - 48k = 2,000 Unfav.
- *Set B Actual:* 28k - 1k = 27,000. *Idle Capacity:* 28k - 22k = 6,000 Unfav.
- *Set C Applied:* Budget (30k) - Idle Cap (-2k) = 32,000.
