Time: 1 hour

In writing a letter message and report the facts should be clear, concise, complete and correct. The writing should be reader oriented as far as possible because the receiver is the main focus of the message. Therefore, the writer should take care of the 7 Cs of communication while writing a message, letter or report. To make the message effective, the writer needs to follow the writing process. The first step of the process is planning which includes determining the purpose, analyzing the audience, gathering the information, and selecting the medium. The second step is drafting which includes organizing, formulating, and editing the message.
