Department of Accounting and Information Systems
(Profile of the Department)
The Department of Accounting and Information Systems is one of the major departments under the
Faculty of Business Studies of the University of Barisal. The University of Barisal, established in 22
February 2011, is an accredited, non-profit institution operating year round and providing Undergraduate,
Graduate and Postgraduate education for meritorious students from all corners of Bangladesh.
The university has approximately 150 teaching staffs and 5,000 students enrolled in 20 Departments
under 6 Faculties. Over the years, the university has built up a reputation for commendable academic
excellence and contributed uniquely to the socio-economic, political and cultural development of the
region.
Faculty of Business studies started functioning from the academic session 2011-2012 with two
departments – Marketing and Management. Accounting and Information Systems was set up from the
academic session 2012-2013. Finance and Banking was set up from the academic session 2013-2014.
The Department of Accounting and Information Systems commenced with 2 teachers and 75 students in
2012 and has swelled to over 350 students and 8 teaching staffs in the present time with the department
upholding its unwavering commitment to first rate teaching quality and teaching staff.
The BBA (Hons.) and MBA programs are to run for four years and one year respectively. The
International schooling requirement of 16 years (10+2+4) of regular study for graduation is also met with
the 4-year BBA (Hons.) program (10+2+4).
Within the framework of the rules of the University, the departmental Academic Committee may adopt
policies for strengthening the academic and other extra-curricular activities of the department. The
departmental Planning and Development Committee consisting of one third of the senior teachers work
as brain of the department, continuously in search of improving things including the logistic support,
recruitment/promotion of faculty and providing guidelines for overall development of the Department
for future.

BBA (Hons.) in Accounting and Information Systems
The BBA in Accounting and Information Systems is a prestigious degree that has been offered by Barisal
University since 2012-2013 academic session. It is internationally recognized qualification for grooming
professionals, business executives, entrepreneurs and academics in the relevant area seeking to expand
their intellectual horizon and improve their management skills and competence. The program is rigorous,
with an emphasis on practicalities and skills for immediate use, which offers students a comprehensive
education in all major facets of business.

BBA in Accounting and Information Systems is a four year composite program of 129 credit hours
following a semester system of learning, examination and evaluation. The curriculum consists of 41
course units of 100 marks each; four oral examination of 25 marks each to be held at the end of each
academic year, equivalent to 1 (one) course, i.e., 100 marks, and an Internship Program of 100 marks
(equivalent to one course) at the end of fourth year. Thus, the whole program has a total mark of 4300,
equivalent to 43 courses of 3 credits each. The courses are spread over eight semesters in four academic
years. For each course unit, there are two lectures every week of one and a half hour duration and each
course unit (including oral examinations and the internship) is weighted 3 (three) credit hours. Thus, the
BBA program is of 129 credit hours.
For each course, there are two in-course tests: each test is of one-hour duration carrying 10 marks. The
duration of the semester final examination is three hours for each course and the mark allocate is 60. To
ensure a very fair evaluation, two independent examiners examine the all final examination scripts. One
of the examiners is usually the course teacher and another one of the examiners is outside the University
(From the Panel of Examiners approved by the Academic Council of the University). In case of a
difference of more than 20% in any script, a third examiner is appointed for evaluation. The total
examination process is administered by a four member examination committee (including one external
member).

Co-Curricular Activities of the Department:
It has been one of the leading departments in the university to take part in all the co-curricular events
organized by the authorities. The departmental volleyball team won championship as well as runner-up
trophies in the inter-departmental volleyball tournament in the past. The departmental table-tennis team
won championship trophy in the inter-departmental table-tennis tournament in the past. The departmental
cricket and football team did better in the inter-departmental cricket and football tournament in the past.
At the cultural arena, the students of the department took pioneering role in setting up debating culture
in the country and creating debating societies throughout Bangladesh. The department arranges study
tours inside the country at regular intervals. The students actively take part in various social and religious
events. At the national days, the students take part in different activities arranged by the university
authority. At the time of fresher’s reception, the students arrange different types of cultural programs.

MGT- 121
History of the Emergence of Bangladesh
60 Hours

Curriculum of the Department of Accounting and Information Systems, University of Barishal

Rationale of the Course:
This course provides an understanding of fundamental and integrated concepts of Bangladesh as a country
as whole. This course is also designed to help the students learn about the historical perspective along with
the language oriented cultural movement in Bangladesh. It has been designed for undergraduate students
to help them learn the rich political scenario of Bangladesh. This course will deepen students’
understanding of complex interconnection of chronological events which led to the construction of
Bangladesh and will improve critical thinking. It will also amplify their sound understanding of history
and politics which will make them responsible Bangladeshi and global citizens as well. The students
attending this course are expected to be made aware of the past and present of Bangladesh for the
enlargement of their knowledge to face the challenges of 21st century.
Intended Learning Objectives (ILO):
The objectives of this courses are1. To equip students with factual knowledge and analytical skills that will enable them to learn and
critically appreciate history, politics and economy of Bangladesh.
2. To learn about the historical perspective along with the language oriented cultural movement in
Bangladesh.
3. To facilitate the students to identify the chronological movements and historical changes that gave
rise to the emergence of Bangladesh.
4. To familiarize with the Declaration of Military Rule, The Growth of Bengali Nationalism & Quest
for Autonomy
5. To familiarize with the Liberation War of 1971
6. To familiarize with the The Regime of Bangabandhu (1972-1975).
7. To make students' understanding clearer and authentic and rational in the nation-building and
state-building process.
8. To boost up the sense and aspiration of patriotism among the learners that can strengthen the
commitment towards nation-building.
Course Learning Outcomes (CLO):
After successful completion of the course, the students will be able to:
1. Know the primary issues about Bangladesh, i.e., Geographical Features, Ancient Janapadas, Ethnic
Composition, Indigenous Communities, Religious Process in Bengal, General Overview of the
Economy, Culture, and Heritage.
2. Get knowledge about the history of Bengal up to the partition of India.
3. Get an understanding about the Growth of Opposition, Language Movements & Attempts to Build
a Democratic Government.
4. Learn about the Declaration of Military Rule, The Growth of Bengali Nationalism & Quest for
Autonomy.
5. Have knowledge about the Liberation War of 1971.
6. Know about The Regime of Bangabandhu (1972-1975).
7. Critically analyze the national and international factors behind the socio-economic development of
Bangladesh and design solutions to meet specific development needs.
8. Develop rational and practical solutions both individually and collectively through a sense of
ownership for the country and communicate those innovative solutions to meet the critical
challenges of contemporary Bangladesh.

1101: Introduction to Financial Accounting
Course Objectives:
The objective of this course is to develop students’ understanding of basic concepts of
accounting, accounting process, and the application of the conceptual framework in such
process in order to develop their abilities to complete the accounting cycle ending with
preparation and presentation of financial statements. This course will also try to equip the
students with the knowledge and ability to understand and analyze the information
communicated through the financial statements.
Intended Learning Outcomes (ILOs):
Upon completion of the course, students should be able to:
1. Explain what accounting is and its purpose and nature.
2. Understand the key sources of regulation of accounting, particularly in Bangladesh.
3. Explain the key components of IASB conceptual framework and their use.
4. Identify the recording process and steps of accounting cycle.
5. Understand the difference between service and merchandising companies, including
inventory recording process of merchandising concern.
6. Understand the system design of accounting; and
7. Prepare statement of financial position, statement of profit or loss and other
comprehensive income and statement of changes in equity.
8. Understand why cash book and bank statement differ and how they are reconciled.

4202: Business Analysis and Valuation
Course Objectives:
The objective of the course is to provide hands-on experience in business analysis using financial
and non-financial information. This course is aimed at all students who expect at some point in
their careers to use financial statements to evaluate the performance, prospects, and value of a
business. This course will be particularly valuable to students who are seekinga career in
consulting, corporate finance, investment banking, hedge funds, or private equity.
Intended Learning Outcomes (ILOs):
Upon Successful completion of this course, students will be able to:
1. Gain a complete knowledge to develop their ability to basic concepts of financial
statement analysis for investment and credit decisions and solving the business
problems by analysis and interpretation of accounting information, supplied mainly
through financial statements.
2. Get a clear idea about a set of accounting information items which constitutes a logical
framework that can be applied to predict both earnings level and growth in earnings,
and together with other accounting fundamentals, can used to predict fundamental value
of shares.
3. Understand the role of accounting rate of return (ARR) in the security valuation process.
4. Demonstrate the underlying interdependency between cash accounting and accrual
accounting in judging the quality of accrual earnings and the culture of earnings
management by firms.
5. Build a career in consulting, corporate finance, investment banking, hedge funds, or
private equity.
6. Explore the role of leverages—both financial leverage and operating leverage—on
profitability and its relation to macroeconomic and monetary policies.
