# Business Communication & Report Writing — Examination (2016-17)

<!-- page 6 -->
## Question 1

**a)** “The single most significant characteristic of human race is the ability to communicate.” Discuss.

**b)** Discuss the importance of effective communication in Business.

**c)** Find out the proper media of communication in the following cases and explain the reason for selecting such media:

**i)** A salesman has to submit a report on the comparative sales figures to his Manager of the last five years.

**ii)** An employee wants to express his regrets to his supervisor over his misconducts.

**iii)** A manager wants to compliment an employee for distinguished performance.

Probable media are: a lengthy paragraph incorporating the information, talk on the phone, presenting in tabular form with diagrams, a written explanation, face to face communication, making a phone call, writing a memo.
