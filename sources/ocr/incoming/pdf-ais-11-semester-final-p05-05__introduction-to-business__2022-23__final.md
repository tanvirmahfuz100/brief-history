# Introduction to Business — Semester Final Examination (2022-23)

**University of Barishal**
**Department of Accounting & Information Systems**
**BBA 1<small>st</small> Year 1<small>st</small> Semester Final Examination, Session 2022-23**
**Course: AIS 1102 (Introduction to Business)**
**Time: 3 Hours, Total Marks: 60**

> (Answer any four questions. Each question has equal weight of marks. Questions should be answered sequentially and in a relevant way. Handwriting must be readable.)

## Question 1 — [03+03+02+03+04=15]
**a)** Explain the concepts of revenue, costs, and profit. What are the five factors of production? [03]

**b)** Why is capitalism growing, communism declining, and socialism still popular? [03]

**c)** What is the difference between demand-pull and cost-push inflation? [02]

**d)** Define the components of the internal and the external business environments. [03]

**e)** Compare and contrast perfect competition and pure monopoly. Why is it rare to find perfect competition? [04]

## Question 2 — [02+04+04+02+03=15]
**a)** Describe the purpose and role of the WTO. What are the roles of the World Bank and the IMF in world trade? [02]

**b)** Explain the concept of countertrade. Discuss several ways that a company can enter international trade. [04]

**c)** Describe several cultural factors that a company involved in international trade should consider. [04]

**d)** What is a sole proprietorship? What are the advantages and disadvantages of sole proprietorship? [02]

**e)** Differentiate between private limited company and public limited company. [03]

## Question 3 — [04+03+03+05=15]
**a)** What do you mean by the "Corporate Social Responsibility"? Point out the area of corporate social responsibility. [04]

**b)** Argue for or against of corporate social responsibility. [03]

**c)** What is the role of top management in organizational ethics? Give an example of legal but irresponsible behavior. [03]

**d)** Discuss the concept of natural trade barriers. Describe several tariff and nontariff barriers to trade. [05]

## Question 4 — [04+04+04+03=15]
**a)** Summarize the advantages and disadvantages of corporations. Which features contribute to the dominance of corporations in the business world? [04]

**b)** Differentiate between a merger and an acquisition. Describe the different types of corporate mergers. [04]

**c)** Define the term *management*. What are the four key functions of managers? What is the difference between efficiency and effectiveness? [04]

**d)** What is the purpose of planning, and what is needed to do it effectively? Identify the unique characteristics of each type of planning. [03]

## Question 5 — [03+03+06+03=15]
**a)** What is the managerial pyramid? Explain the managerial function of organizing. [03]

**b)** Describe the control process. How can managers empower employees? [03]

**c)** What are the three types of managerial roles? Give examples of things managers might do when acting in each of the different types of roles. [06]

**d)** Define the basic managerial skills. How important is each of these skill sets at the different levels of the management pyramid? [03]
