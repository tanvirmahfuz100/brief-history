4. **a)** In most incentive wage plans, does production above standard reduce labor cost per unit of output? Discuss. [02]
   **b)** Employees of Patron Enterprises work in groups of five plus a group leader. Standard production is 800 units for a 40-hour week. Workers are paid Tk. 6 an hour until production reaches 800 units, then a bonus Tk. 1.50 is paid for production over 800 units, with Tk. 1.20 divided equally among the five workers and the remainder passing to the group leader. Factory overhead is Tk. 6 per direct labor hour and includes the leader’s earnings. Weekly output: Sunday 144 units, Monday 162, Tuesday 190, Wednesday 204, Thursday 204; each 40 hours. Compute the week’s group earnings excluding leader, labor cost per unit, overhead cost per unit, conversion cost per unit, and prepare a daily schedule. [08]
   **c)** A worker takes 75 hours for a job allowed 90 hours. Daily rate Tk. 3.50 per hour. Calculate work cost under piece-rate, time-rate, Halsey premium bonus and Rowan premium wages. Material cost Tk. 150; factory overhead 150% of direct wages. [05]
