## Question 1 — [3+4+4+4=15]
**a)** Define integers, prime numbers, real numbers, rational numbers, irrational numbers, and imaginary number with example. [3]

**b)** Prove that \(\sqrt{2}\) is an irrational number. [4]

**c)** Simplify \(\dfrac{2^{m+3}\times 3^{2m-n}\times 5^{m+n+3}\times 6^{n+1}}{6^{m+1}\times 10^{n+3}\times 15^{m}}\) [3]

**d)** Simplify:

**i.** \(4\dfrac{1}{3}+3\dfrac{1}{5}-2\dfrac{1}{3}\)

**ii.** \(2\dfrac{1}{3}\div\dfrac{2}{5}+\dfrac{2}{5}\times\dfrac{1}{4}\div\dfrac{3}{4}-\dfrac{18}{24}\div 3\)

**[Marks = 3+4+4+4=15]**

## Question 2 — [3+2+5+5=15]
**a)** Define the following and give an example of each: i) Subset of a set ii) Complement of a set iii) Union of two sets iv) Intersection of two sets v) Disjoint set [3]

**b)** Let A={a,b}, B={p,q} and C={q,r}, find i) \((A\times B)\cup(A\times C)\) ii) \((A\times B)\cap(A\times C)\) [2]

**c)** Out of 880 boys in a school, 224 played cricket, 240 played hockey, and 336 played basketball; of the total 64 played both basketball and hockey; 80 played cricket and basketball, and 40 played cricket and hockey; 24 boys played all the three games. How many boys did not play any game, and how many played only one game? [5]

**d)** Out of total 150 students who appeared for CMA examination from a centre, 45 failed in accounts, 50 failed in maths and 30 failed in costing. Those who failed both in accounts and maths were 30, those who failed both in maths and costings were 32 and those who failed both in accounts and costing were 35. The student who failed in all the three subjects were 25. Find out the number who failed at least in any one of the subjects. [5]

**[Marks = 3+2+5+5=15]**

## Question 3 — [3+3+3+3+3=15]
**a)** Find the values of-

**i.** \(\log_{8} 25\)

**ii.** \(\log_{4} 256\)

hint. \(\log_{10} 2 = 0.3010\) [3]

**b)** Prove that \(\dfrac{1}{\log_{6}24}+\dfrac{1}{\log_{12}24}+\dfrac{1}{\log_{8}24}=2\) [3]

**c)** If \(x=\left(3^{\frac{1}{3}}+3^{-\frac{1}{3}}\right)\), show that \(3x^{3}-9x=10\). [3]

**d)** A man wishes to have Tk. 1,50,000 available in a bank account when his daughter's first year college expenses begin. How much must he deposit now at 12% compounded annually if the girl is to start in college five years from now? [3]

**e)** A machine depreciates at the rate of 10% of its value at the beginning of a year. The machine was purchased for Tk. 5,810 and the scrap value realized when sold was Tk. 2,250. Find the number of years that the machine was used. [3]

**[Marks = 3+3+3+3+3=15]**

## Question 4 — [3+3+3+3+3=15]
**a)** Define permutations and combinations with its required formula.

**b)** Find how many four-letters words can be formed out of the following words-

**i.** EQUATION          **ii.** TRIANGLE

**c)** A store has 8 regular doors and 5 emergency doors which can be opened only from the inside. In how many ways can a person enter and leave the store?

**d)** Six officials of a company are to fly to a conference in Dhaka. Company policy states that no two can fly on the same plane. If there are 9 flights available, how many flight schedules can be established?

**e)** A cricket team of 11 players is to be seated for pre-match discussion. In how many ways can they seat themselves around a table if the captain and wicket-keeper choose to sit together?

**[Marks = 3+3+3+3+3=15]**

## Question 5 — [3+4+4+4=15]
**a)** Find the last term and sum of the series:

\(1+\dfrac{4}{3}+\dfrac{5}{3}+\) ........... to 58 terms

**b)** Find four numbers in Arithmetic progression, whose sum is 20 and the sum of whose squares is 120.

**c)** The first and the last terms of an A.P. are respectively -4 and 146, and the sum of A.P. = 7171. Find the number of terms of the A.P. and also its common difference.

**d)** B arranges to pay off a debt of TK 9600 in 48 annual instalments which forms an arithmetic series. When 40 of these instalments are paid, B becomes insolvent and his creditor finds that TK 2,400 still remains unpaid. Find the value of each of the first three instalments of B.

**[Marks =3+4+4+4=15]**

## Question 6 — [4+3+4+4=15]
**a)** Solve the following simultaneous equations:

**i.** \(3p^{2}-7pq+4q^{2}=0,\; 4p+3q=5\)

**ii.** \(m^{2}+n^{2}=185,\; m-n=3\)

**b)** The demand and supply equations are \(2p^{2}+q^{2}=11\) and \(p+2q=7\). Find the equilibrium price and quantity, where \(p\) stands for price and \(q\) for quantity.

**c)** A commodity is produced by using 3 units of labour and 2 units of capital, the total cost comes to 62. If the commodity is produced by using 4 units of labour and 1 unit of capital, the cost comes to 56. What is the cost per unit of labour and capital?

**d)** Three types of microcomputer, types A, B and C, are used in a university. In January the Physics department purchased 3 microcomputers of type A, 4 of type B and 2 of type C at a cost of \$4,600. At the same time the Chemistry Department purchased 2 microcomputers of type A, 3 of type B and 1 of type C at a total cost of \$3,000. On 1<small>st</small> February the cost of type A microcomputers was increased by 20% and the cost of type C microcomputers was increased by \$50. The cost of type B remained unchanged. In February the Mathematics Department purchased 5 microcomputers of type A, 10 of type B and 10 of type C at a total cost of \$14,900. Calculate the cost of each type of microcomputer in January.

**[Marks =4+3+4+4=15]**
