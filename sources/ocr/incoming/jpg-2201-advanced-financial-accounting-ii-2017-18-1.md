## Question 3
**(a)** Define warrant. Explain the accounting for convertible preferred stock.
**(b)** On January 1, 2015 Maiza Inc. granted stock options to officers and key employees for purchase of 20,000 shares of the company's Tk. 10 per common stock at Tk. 25 per share. The option was exercisable within a 5-year period beginning from 1<sup>st</sup> January 2017, by grantees still in the employ of the company, and expiring December 31, 2021. The service period for this award is 2 years. Assuming that the fair-value option pricing model determines total compensation expense to be Tk. 350,000. On April 1, 2016, 2,000 options were terminated when the employees resigned from the company. The market price of the common stock was Tk. 35 on this date. On March 1, 2017, 2,000 options were exercised when the market price of the common stock is Tk. 40 per share.

**Instruction:** Prepare journal entries to record issuance of the stock options, termination of the stock options, changes to compensation expense, for the year ended December 31, 2015, 2016 and 2017.

**(c)** Jaman Inc. uses a calendar year for financial reporting. The company is authorized to issue 9,000,000 shares of Tk. 10 par common stock. At no time Jaman is sued any potentially Diluted securities. Listed below is a summary of Jaman's common share activities.
1. No. of common share issued and outstanding at December 31, 2012 — 2,000,000
2. Share issues as a result of 10% stock dividend on September 30, 2013 — 200,000
3. Share issues for cash on March 31, 2014 — 2,000,000
4. As 2 for 1 stock split of Jaman's common stock took place on March 31, 2015.

**Instructions:**
**(i)** Compute the weighted-average number of shares used in computing Earnings Per Common share for 2013 on the 2014 comparative income statement.
**(ii)** Compute the weighted-average number of shares used in computing Earnings Per Common share for 2014 on the 2014 comparative income statement.
**(iii)** Compute the weighted-average number of shares used in computing Earnings Per Common share for 2014 on the 2015 comparative income statement.
**(iv)** Compute the weighted-average number of shares used in computing Earnings Per Common share for 2015 on the 2015 comparative income statement. [3+5+7]
