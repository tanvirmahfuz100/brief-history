## Question 2 — [3]
ABC company charges its customers according to their usage of electricity. One rate it charges a particular class of user is as follows: a basic monthly charge of \$ 3.35, 4.129 cents per KWH for the first 350 KWH, and 6.882 cents per KWH for any KWH in excess of 350.

**Requirements:**

**i)** Determine the cost function and graph it. **ii)** What are the total and average charges for using 300 kwh? **iii)** For using 500 kwh **iv)** How many kwh were used if the total charge was \$131.35?
