# 1102 — Introduction to Business (3 credits)

**Semester.** # 1101 — Introduction to Financial Accounting (3 credits)

**Source.** AIS BBA Curriculum — 2022-23 (11th Batch), University of Barishal, Department of Accounting & Information Systems.

## Course objectives

The course is intended to enhance students’ understanding of business, the business environment, the functional areas of an organization, and the role business plays in today’s society. Students will acquire a broad-based knowledge of business and the business Environment.

## Intended learning outcomes

- Understand the fundamental concepts, theories, and models in carrying out business and ethical management.
- Demonstrate a vocabulary of business terms.
- Illustrate the evolution of business from the feudalism to modern capitalism.
- Distinguish different forms of business and compreh7end the roles of differentstakeholders.
- Formulate the vision and mission of a business.
- Examine the PESTEL factors and gain a basic understanding of competition.
- Explore the activities of different functional areas of a business.
- Develop an understanding of socially responsible business practices.
- Identify the relevance of international business in modern era.
- Gain a basic understanding of the nature of entrepreneurship.
- Realize the importance of global and regional business associations.

## Detailed contents

- The nature of business: [ILO 1, ILO 2] Definition of Business; Vision; Mission; Indicators of A Successful Business; Customer Needs; Quality Products and Services; The Challenges and Complexities of Today‘s Business; Forms of Business Ownership; MNCs.
- The Evolution of Business: [ILO 3] Feudalism: Land, Labour and Property Rights; Money, Capital and Commerce; Mercantilism: Trade and Enterprise; The Growth of Enterprise; Industrial Revolution: First to Fourth IR; The Changing Forms of Business Organization.
- The Stakeholders: [ILO 4] Stockholders; Board of Directors (BOD); Management; Government; Community.
- Business Environment:
- 1 The Impact of External Forces: [ILO 2, ILO 6] Political forces; Economic Forces; Social Forces; Technological Forces; Natural Environment and Business; Legal Forces; Competition; Porter‘s Five Forces Model; Opportunities and Threats.
- 2 The Internal Environment: [ILO 2, ILO 7] Accounting & Finance; Marketing; Human Resource Management; Production/Operation; Management Information Systems; Research & Development; Porter‘s Generic Value Chain; Strengths and Weaknesses; An Introduction to SWOT.
- Management: [ILO 2, ILO 7] Definition of Management; Decision making; Planning; Organizing; Leading; Motivating; Controlling.
- Marketing: [ILO 2, ILO 7] Definition of Marketing; Product; Pricing; Promotion; Place.
- Finance: [ILO 2, ILO 7] Equity vs. Debt; Financial Markets; Stock Market; Blockchain/ Fintech.
- International Business: [ILO 2, ILO 9] Introduction and overview of international business; Environmental variables of culture, politics, and economics; Entry strategies for new markets and countries; International trade and foreign direct investment; Global outsourcing and its role in international operations.777
- Ethics and Social Responsibility of Business: [ILO 2, ILO 8] Ethics; Business ethics; The Need for Business Ethics; Ethical Dilemma; Corporate Social Responsibility; Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs); Social Business.
- Entrepreneurship: [ILO 2, ILO 10] Definition of Entrepreneurs and Entrepreneurship; Types of Entrepreneurs; Advantages and Disadvantages of Being an entrepreneur; Characteristics of a Successful Entrepreneur; Start-ups; Franchising.
- Global, Regional and Local Business Associations: [ILO 2, ILO 11] Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC); Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN); Bangladesh Garments Manufacturers‘ Association (BGMEA); BRICS; Federation of Bangladesh Chamber of Commerce and Industries (FBCCI); European Union (EU); Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development (OECD);South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation (SAARC); World Trade Organization (WTO).
