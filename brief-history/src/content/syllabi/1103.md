## Course objectives

This course attempts to develop the skill of students in exchanging messages with others in order to develop a mutual understanding of business deals. The students, with their professional oral and written communication skills, will be able to build the expertise needed to succeed in today's technologically-superior workplaces.

## Intended learning outcomes

- Comprehend the significance of business communication in today's technology-driven workplace.
- Understand ethical, legal, cultural, and global issues influencing business communication.
- Prepare letters, e-mails, memos, and reports persuasively.
- Communicate successfully with colleagues and business clients in in-person and online meetings.
- Deliver oral presentations in a proficient manner.
- Prepare for and participate in job interviews.
