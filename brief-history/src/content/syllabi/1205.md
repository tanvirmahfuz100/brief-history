## Course objectives

The objective of this course is to introduce the students to the basic concepts of computers and information systems. The students will be exposed to the use of operating system and the widely used office and application software/packages. The course will also cover the fundamental concepts, theories, practices, and management of information systems in organizations.

## Intended learning outcomes

- To understand the basic knowledge of a computer system.
- Understand the implications of Information Technology (IT) in businesses.
- Be able to identify and operate the basic hardware of a computer system.
- Be able to identify and operate the basic software of a computer system.
- Comprehend the underlying concepts of Computer Communications and Network.
- Understand the operational functions of E-Commerce and the impact of Social ECommerce and M-Commerce in business.
- Understand the implications of Information Systems (IS) in businesses world.
