## Question 2 — [15]

**a)** Describe the five basic assumptions that underlie the financial accounting structure. [05]

**b)** What are the enhancing qualities of the qualitative characteristics? What is the role of enhancing qualities in the conceptual framework? [04]

**c)** Use the expanded accounting equation to answer each of the following questions: [4.5]

**(i)** The liabilities of Cai Company are $90,000. Meiyu Cai's capital account is $150,000; drawings are $40,000; revenues, $450,000; and expenses, $320,000. What is the amount of Cai Company's total assets?

**(ii)** The total assets of Pereira Company are $57,000. Karen Perry's capital account is $25,000; drawings are $7,000; revenues, $50,000; and expenses, $35,000. What is the amount of the company's total liabilities?

**(iii)** The total assets of Yap Co. are $600,000 and its liabilities are equal to two-thirds of its total assets. What is the amount of Yap Co.'s owner's equity?

**d)** Indicate whether each of the three statements presented below is true or false. [1.5]

**i.** The three steps in the accounting process are identification, recording, and examination.

**ii.** The two most common types of external users are investors and creditors.

**iii.** The cost principle dictates that companies record assets at their cost and continue to report them at their cost over the time the asset is held.
