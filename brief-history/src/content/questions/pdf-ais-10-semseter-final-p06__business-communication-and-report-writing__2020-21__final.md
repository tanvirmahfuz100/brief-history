## Question 1 — [4+4+4+3] = [15]

**(a).** What is communication? Describe the communication process.

**(b).** Explain the importance of effective communications in modern business.

**(c).** "Some short-words are hard, some long words are easy. Thus the suggestion to prefer short words doesn't make sense" – Discuss.

**(d).** What are the barriers of effective communication?

## Question 2 — [5+8+2] = [15]

**(a).** Listening skill in communication is one of the important factors that make communication effective. Briefly mention the ways of improving listening skills.

**(b).** In public speaking you need to focus on your dressing, posture, gestures, manners, vocabulary and choice of words. Discuss all these elements in detail.

**(c).** Briefly discuss the Jargon in a communication process.

## Question 3 — [4+4+4+3] = [15]

**(a).** Briefly explain different types of letters that are used in business.

**(b).** What is a *Press Release*? When and why a business firm does use it?

**(c).** What is a circular? Is there any difference between a Circular and an Advertisement? Explain.

**(d).** When and why does a business firm use a *Memo*?

## Question 4 — [6+5+4] = [15]

**(a).** What are the common elements of an email? Explain the rules of etiquette that apply to email in both personal and business contexts.

**(b).** You are the branch head of a private commercial bank. Your supervising manager has instructed you to find out a suitable place to operate a sub-branch. Accordingly, you have visited and inspected your surroundings. Upon inspection, you have selected an area. Now, write an email to your supervising manager that will convince him to establish the sub-branch at your chosen area.

**(c).** In business communication, an email might not always be a good means of communication. Explain.
