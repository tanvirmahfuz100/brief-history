## Question 2 — [3+3+3+3+3=15]

**(a)** Define the following with examples: Null set; Universal set; Intersection of sets; Complement of a set.

**(b)** If $p = 2 + 2^{\frac{2}{3}} + 2^{\frac{1}{3}}$, find the value of $p^3 - 6p^2 + 6p - 2$

**(c)** If A= {1, 2, 3, 4}, B= {2, 4, 6}, C= {1, 2, 5} and U= {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8}. Compute the following:

**i.** $A' \cap (B' \cap C')$

**ii.** $A \times (B \cup C)'$

**iii.** $(A' \cup B') \cap C'$

**iv.** $A \times (B \times C)$

**(d)** A company has examined its cost structure and revenue structure and has determined that C is the total cost, R is the total revenue and X is the number of units produced are related as $C = 100+0.015x^2$ and $R = 3x$.

**i.** Find the production rate X that will maximize profits of the company.

**ii.** Find the profit when X = 120

**(e)** A survey was conducted of a group of 1000 successful investors. The results indicated that 496 owned utilities, 510 owned company stock, 540 owned govt. securities. While 214 owned utility and company stock, 300 owned utility and govt. securities, 440 owned company stock and govt. securities while 200 owned all three. Draw a Venn diagram to illustrate this. What set is empty? How many investors did not invest in any one of the above?

## Question 3 — [3+4+4+4=15]

**(a)** Find the values of-

**i.** $\log_8 25$

**ii.** $\log_4 256$

hint. $\log_{10} 2 = 0.3010$

**(b)** Prove that $\frac{1}{\log_6 24} + \frac{1}{\log_{12} 24} + \frac{1}{\log_8 24} = 2$

**(c)** A man wishes to have Tk. 1,50,000 available in a bank account when his daughter's first-year college expenses begin. How much must he deposit now at 12% compounded annually if the girl is to start in college five years from now?

**(d)** A machine depreciates at the rate of 10% of its value at the beginning of a year. The machine was purchased for Tk. 5,810 and the scrap value realized when sold was Tk. 2,250. Find the number of years that the machine was used.

## Question 4 — [2+3+4+4+3=15]

**(a)** Define permutations and combinations with its required formula. [2]

**(b)** An examination paper which is divided into two groups consisting of 3 and 4 questions respectively, carries the note. "It is not necessary to answer all the questions. One question must be answered from each group." In how many ways can an examinee select the questions? [3]

**(c)** Out of 5 males and 6 females, a committee of 5 is to be formed. Find the number of ways in which it can be done so that among the persons chosen in the committee there are (i) 3 males and 2 females, (ii) 2 males, (iii) no females, (iv) at least one female, (v) not more than 3 males. [4]

**(d)** A store has 8 regular doorways and 5 emergency doors which can be opened only from the inside. In how many ways can a person enter and leave the store? [4]

**(e)** Six officials of a company are to fly to a conference in Dhaka. Company policy states that no two can fly on the same plane. If there are 9 flights available, how many flight schedules can be established? [3]

## Question 5 — [3+3+3+3+3=15]

**(a)** Define equation. Distinguish between linear equation and quadratic equation. [3]

**(b)** A commodity is produced by using 3 units of labour and 2 units of capital. The total cost comes to 62. If the commodity is produced by using 4 units of labour and 1 unit of capital, the cost comes to 56. What is the cost per unit of labour and capital? [3]

**(c)** Solve the following simultaneous equations: [3]

**i.** $3p^2 - 7pq + 4q^2 = 0, 4p + 3q = 5$

**ii.** $m^2 + n^2 = 185, m - n = 3$

**(d)** The demand and supply equations are $2p^2 + q^2 = 11$ and $p + 2q = 7$. Find the equilibrium price and quantity, where p stands for price and q for quantity. [3]

**(e)** Three types of microcomputer, types A, B and C, are used in a university. In January the Physics department purchased 3 microcomputers of type A, 4 of type B and 2 of type C at a cost of $4,600. At the same time the Chemistry Department purchased 2 microcomputers of type A, 3 of type B and 1 of type C at a total cost of $3,000.

On 1st February the cost of type A microcomputers was increased by 20% and the cost of type C microcomputers was increased by $50. The cost of type B remained unchanged.

In February the Mathematics Department purchased 5 microcomputers of type A, 10 of type B and 10 of type C at a total cost of $14,900.

Calculate the cost of each type of microcomputer in January. [3]

## Question 6 — [4+4+5+2=15]

**(a)** What do you mean by Sequence and Series? Differentiate between A.P and G.P. [4]

**(b)** The sum of the first 10 terms of an A.P is 30 and the sum of the first 40 terms is 90. Find the first term of the A.P and the sum of first 50 terms. [4]

**(c)** A geometric progression has positive terms. The sum of the first six terms is nine times the sum of the first three terms. The seventh term is 320. Find the common ratio and the first term. Find the smallest value of n such that the sum to first n terms of the progression exceeds $10^6$. [5]

**(d)** The first term of an arithmetic sequence is equal to 200 and the common difference is equal to -10. Find the value of the 20th term. [2]
