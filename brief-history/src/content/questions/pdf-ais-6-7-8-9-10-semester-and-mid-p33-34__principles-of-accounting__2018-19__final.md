# Principles of Accounting — Final Examination (2018-19)

1. **a)** Briefly explain the objectives of financial reporting. [05]
   **b)** Briefly explain the qualitative characteristic of accounting information. [05]
   **c)** Explain the different measurement bases of determining the monetary amounts at which the elements of the financial statements are to be recognized and carried in the balance sheet and income statement. Which one is most commonly used? Why? [05]
2. **a)** Define journal. Why is it referred as primary book of accounting? [05]
   **b)** Hafsa has opened a hotel business on September 1, 2018. During the first month:
   Sep. 1 Invested Tk. 30,000 as cash and 20,000 in the form of equipment.
   2 Company paid 5,000 as rent for the month.
   3 Purchased furniture for Tk. 20,000, paying 8,000 in cash and issued a note for the balance amount.
   10 Paid 500 for miscellaneous expense.
   15 Received a bill for advertisement Tk. 300.
   18 Withdrew Tk. 500 for personal use.
   30 The company received Tk. 10,500 for service rendered in the form of accommodation.
   **Instructions:** Journalize the September transactions; open ledger account and post the September transactions; prepare a trial balance at September 30, 2018.
3. Sadaf started his own delivery service, Sadaf services, on June 1, 2017. Transactions:
   June 1 Sadaf invested Tk. 30,000 cash in his business.
   2 Purchased a van for delivery for Tk. 10,000; paid 3,000 in cash and signed a note payable for the remaining balance.
   4 Paid 1,000 for office rent for the month.
   6 Performed services of Tk. 5,000 on account.
   8 Withdrew Tk. 300 for personal use.
   10 Purchased supplies for Tk. 2,000 on account.
   12 Received a cash payment of Tk. 2,000 for the service provided on June 6.
   15 Purchased gasoline for Tk. 200 on account.
   18 Made a cash payment of Tk. 1,000 on the note payable.
   22 Paid 200 for utilities.
   24 Paid 200 for gasoline purchased on account.
   30 Paid 1,200 for employee salaries.
   **Requirements:** Show the effects of the previous transactions on the accounting equation; prepare an income statement for June; prepare a balance sheet at June 30, 2017.
