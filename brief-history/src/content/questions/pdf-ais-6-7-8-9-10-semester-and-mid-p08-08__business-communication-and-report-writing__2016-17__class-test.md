# Business Communication & Report Writing — Class Test (2016-17)

<!-- page 8 -->
## Question 1 — [05]
Assume that you are the assistant manager of a hotel and are describing your hotel's meeting room to a prospective customer who is thinking of holding a seminar there. Turn the following piece of information into coherent writing, making paragraph breaks where you think appropriate. Be ready to explain why you grouped the information and managed the emphasis the way you did. You may need to add some words or information to make the facts flow smoothly.

| | |
|---|---|
| 1 We have a meeting room. | 9 The lounge is at the opposite end of the hotel from the meeting room. |
| 2 It will be available on the date you requested. | 10 The meeting room has a lectern. |
| 3 It can seat 100 people. | 11 It has a projector. |
| 4 The seating can be managed to your specifications. | 12 It has a screen. |
| 5 It is quite. | 13 It has a laptop hookup. |
| 6 It is on the ground floor. | 14 We can rent additional equipment. |
| 7 It is not near to the guest rooms. | 15 We can rent it at no charge to you. |
| 8 The lounge has live music on occasion. | 16 The charge for the room is Tk. 30,000 |
| | 17 This is the charge for one day. |
