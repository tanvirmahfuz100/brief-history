# Principles of Marketing — Final (2019-20)

<!-- page 1 -->
> Answer any four set of questions. Each part of the questions must be written sequentially

**Time-** 3 hours     **Full Marks-** 60

## Question 1 — [07+04+04]
**(a)** Identify the key elements of a customer driven marketing strategy and discuss the marketing management orientations that guide marketing strategy. [07]
**(b)** "Companies should manage customer equity". Explain the concept in light with customer relationship group. [04]
**(c)** Discuss how to design business portfolios and develop growth strategies. [04]

## Question 2 — [10+05]
**(a)** Suppose you are a strategic decision maker of a renowned corporation. You are required to deliver a suitable marketing plan for the company. What are the factors you should consider in designing a successful marketing plan? What are the challenges you may face? Develop a sustainable marketing plan and explain in details. [10]
**(b)** Identify the major trends in the firm's natural and technological environments. [05]

## Question 3 — [05+05+05]
**(a)** Which of the four sets of factors affecting consumer behavior most strongly affects consumers' purchase decision when shopping at goodwill? Explain. [05]
**(b)** Explain how the market structure and demand differ for business markets compare to consumer markets. [05]
**(c)** Compare the institutional and government markets and explain how institutional and government buyers make their buying decisions. [05]

## Question 4 — [06+04+05]
**(a)** As a marketing executive, how will you identify attractive market segments? How will you formulate a market-targeting strategy? [06]
**(b)** List and discuss the major bases for segmenting consumer and business markets. [04]
**(c)** "Branding poses challenging decisions to the marketer" Do you agree? What initiatives you might undertake to create a strong brand image? [05]

## Question 5 — [05+05+05]
**(a)** What is price? Distinguish between value-based pricing and cost-based pricing. [05]
**(b)** "Companies can choose between two broad strategies: market-skimming pricing and market-penetration pricing". Explain. [05]
**(c)** Discuss the key issues related to initiating price changes. [05]

## Question 6 — [06+06+03]
**(a)** "Advertising strategy consists of two major elements: creating advertising messages and selecting advertising media". Explain. [06]
**(b)** Identify and explain the six major sales force management steps. [06]
**(c)** What are the factors to be considered in developing the sales promotion program? [03]
