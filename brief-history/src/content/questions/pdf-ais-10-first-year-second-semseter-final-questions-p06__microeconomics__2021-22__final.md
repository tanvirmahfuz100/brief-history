# Microeconomics — Semester Final (2021-22)

<!-- page 6 -->
> Answer any four sets of questions. Each part of the questions must be written sequentially.

**Time:** 3 Hours     **Course Code:** AIS 1202     **Course Name:** Microeconomics.     **Full Marks:** 60

## Question 1 — [07+04+04]
**a)** Differentiate between micro and macroeconomics? Why do you believe that studying microeconomics is important for you though your concentration is accounting and information systems? [07]
**b)** Discuss the following statement with a suitable example and graph "As consumption increases, the marginal utility derived from each additional unit declines." [04]
**c)** Illustrate the consumer's equilibrium with a single commodity. [04]

## Question 2 — [05+06+04]
**a)** Define utility. Explain Marginal, Average, and Total utility and their relationships. [05]
**b)** Is change in supply and shift in supply the same? If not, then discuss the respective factors for each. [06]
**c)** What is Giffen goods? Discuss its differences with inferior goods. [04]

## Question 3 — [05+05+05]
**a)** What do you mean by Demand? Illustrate the factors affecting demand for tea. [05]
**b)** Suppose that you know that consumers' incomes have gone up, and that an advance in technology has lowered the cost of making computers. Assuming that a computer is a normal good, what will happen to the price and quantity of computers as a result of the above two changes? [05]
**c)** What do you mean by elasticity of demand? Discuss factors Affecting Elasticity of Demand. [05]

## Question 4 — [05+05+05]
**a)** Distinguish between movement along supply curve and shift in supply curve. [05]
**b)** Enumerate supply function and supply schedule. How elasticity of supply is determined? [05]
**c)** Find the equilibrium price and quantity from the given demand and supply function. Demand function:

$$3P+Q^2+5Q-102=0$$

and

Supply function:

$$P-2Q^2+3Q+71=0$$ [05]

## Question 5 — [05+05+05]
**a)** Explain the concept of Production Possibility frontier. [05]
**b)** Define the iso-cost line and write the properties of the iso-cost line. Find the slope of the iso-cost line. [05]
**c)** The cost equation for Sweet Sugars Limited is $C_1= 75Q_1-8Q_1^2+Q_1^3-85$. It sells in a perfectly competitive market. The sugar industry's demand and supply curves are $Q=360-2P$ and $Q=10+3P$ respectively. Here P and Q are the price and quantity demanded/supplied and $C_1$ and $Q_1$ are the total cost and quantity supplied by the firm. Find out the:
**i)** Equilibrium price and quantity of sugar;
**ii)** Profit maximizing output of Sweet sugar; and
**iii)** Profit made by Sweet Sugar in the short-run. [05]

## Question 6 — [05+05+05]
**a)** Discuss the main features of the following firm's structure in short:
**i)** Perfect competition
**ii)** Monopoly
**iii)** Oligopoly [05]
**b)** What do you mean by monopolistic competition? Explain the equilibrium of a firm under monopolistic competition. [05]
**c)** Distinguish between price discrimination and single-price monopoly. Which one is better for a firm? Show graphically. [05]
