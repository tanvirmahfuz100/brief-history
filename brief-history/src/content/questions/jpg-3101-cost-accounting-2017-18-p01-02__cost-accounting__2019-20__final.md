## Question 5 — [2+8+5]
**(a)** In most incentive wage plans, does production above standard reduce the labor cost per unit of output? Discuss. [02]
**(b)** Employees of Patron enterprises work in groups of five, plus a group leader. Standard production for a group is 800 units for a 40-hour week. The workers are paid Tk. 6 an hour until production reaches 800 units; then a bonus Tk. 1.50 is paid for production over 800 units, with Tk. 1.20 divided equally among the five workers and reminder passing to the group leader (Who is also paid weekly salary of Tk. 300). Factory overhead is Tk. 6 per direct labor hour and includes the group leader's earnings. The production record of a group for one week shows: [08]

| Day | Units | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday | 144 | 40 |
| Monday | 162 | 40 |
| Tuesday | 190 | 40 |
| Wednesday | 204 | 40 |
| Thursday | 204 | 40 |

**Required:**
**(i)** Compute the week's earnings of the group (excluding the leader), the labor cost per unit, the overhead cost per unit, and the conversion cost per unit, based upon the above data and bonus plan. (Round off unit costs to four decimal places.)
**(ii)** Prepare a schedule showing daily earnings of the group (excluding the leader), unit labor cost, unit overhead cost, and the conversion cost per unit, assuming that the company uses the group bonus plan.

**(c)** A worker takes 75 hours to do a job for which the time allowed is 90 hours. His daily rate is Tk 3.50 per hour. Calculate the works cost of the job under the following methods of payment of wages; (i) Piece –rate (ii) Time rate (iii) Halsey premium bonus scheme (iv) Rowan premium [05]
**Additional information:**
**(i)** Material cost Tk. 150
**(ii)** Factory overhead 150% of direct wages.
