Department of Accounting and Information Systems, University of Barisal
1st Year 1st Semester Final Examination -2019. Session: 2018-19 (Admission Session: 2018-19)
Course Code: AIS 1103. Course Title: Business Communication and Report Writing

Time: 3 Hours. Full Marks: 60
Answer any Four Questions.

1. a) "Communication is the sum of all things a person does when he wants to create understanding in the mind of another. It is a bridge of meaning. It involves a systematic and continuous process of telling, listening and understanding."-Discuss. 5
   b) On what aspects does an organization need external information? What can be the sources of this information? What precautions should be taken while accepting external information? 5
   c) What is the importance of advice as an objective of communication? 5

2. a) What are the various forms of written communication? Discuss the relative merits and limitations of written communication. 5
   b) What are the 7 Cs of effective oral communication? Discuss the relevance of them. 5
   c) "Downward channel of communication may be the most commonly used, but it is also the one most inadequate and unsatisfactory."-Do you agree? 5

3. a) What is a memorandum? How does a memorandum different from a letter? 5
   b) Draft an office order regarding the transfer of an employee from head office to take over the independent charge of a unit. 5
   c) Mention the different parts of a good business letter and draw a diagram indicating the position of each part in the letter. 5

4. a) Define business letter. What are the functions of a business letter? 5
   b) Describe the mechanical structure of a letter. 5
   c) Define enquiries. What should be considered at the time of drafting an enquiry? 5

5. a) Define business report. What are the types of business report. 3
   b) Define short and long proposal. What are the parts of a short proposal and format of a long proposal? 3
   c) Distinguish between agenda and minutes. What should be considered at the time of writing minutes? 3
   d) What is public relation letter? What are the hints for drafting public relations letter? 3
   e) Define advertisement. What are the characteristics of good advertisement? 3

6. Write short notes on the following topics: 15
   a) Grapevine
   b) Agenda and Minutes
   c) Statutory Report
   d) Paralanguage
   e) Indexing

-The End-
