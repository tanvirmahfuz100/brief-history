Department of Accounting and Information Systems, University of Barishal
1st Year 1st Semester Final Examination, Session: 2019-20 (Admission Session: 2019-20)
Course Code: AIS 1103. Course Title: Business Communication and Report Writing
Time: 3 Hours. Full Marks: 60

Answer any Four Questions.

1. a) "Communication is the life blood of business organization." Discuss. Explain any two ways in which effective communication benefits your organization and two ways in which it benefits you personally. [5]
   b) What do you think can be the major objectives of communication? Write short notes on various kinds of orders. [5]
   c) How would you distinguish between oral communication and face to face communication? What are the advantages of face to face communication? [5]

2. a) Distinguish between the formal and informal channel of communication. Why is it necessary for an organization to have an informal channel of communication? [5]
   b) What are the semantic barriers to effective communication? How can these barriers be overcome? [5]
   c) "Downward channel of communication may be the most commonly used, but it is also the one most inadequate and unsatisfactory." Do you agree? [5]

3. a) How do the personal opinions and prejudices of various individuals act as barriers to effective communication? Illustrate your answer with suitable examples. [4]
   b) Briefly discuss the importance of learning non-verbal communication skills. [4]
   c) Define soft skill. How to develop the soft skill of a person? [4]
   d) Distinguish between intrapersonal communication and interpersonal communication. What is the importance of interpersonal communication? [3]

4. a) What is a memorandum? How does a memorandum different from a letter? [5]
   b) Draft an office order regarding the transfer of an employee from head office to take over the independent charge of a unit. [5]
   c) Place an order for certain goods to an imaginary company which you reserve the right to reject if delivered after more than 10 days of the date of order. State that they are to send by parcel post. [5]

5. Messrs. Hiremath Bros. wish to place an order with National Paints Co. Ltd. for a few types of paints, vanish and mixers. Prepare an order for them mentioning that the tins should be sent by passenger train, carriage forward, to your station, where you will collect them. Mention also the method of payment. [5]
   You have received the first order from a customer who does not mention any mode of payment. Write a reply, requesting him to send cash or to furnish references if he wants an account opened. [5]
   Even though you were assured of delivery of goods within three weeks of placing the order, it is six weeks from the date of the order and you have yet to receive them. Write a letter to your supplier, pointing out the delay and specifying a date by which you wish to get the goods. (delay in the execution of the order). [5]

6. Write short notes on any five of the following: [15]
   a) Grapevine
   b) Body language
   c) Oral communication
   d) Horizontal and vertical filing
   e) Audio-Visual communication
   f) Indexing
