# Introduction to Business — Semester Final Examination (2019-20)

<!-- page 24 -->

Department of Accounting and Information Systems, University of Barishal
1st Year 1st Semester Final Examination, Session: 2019-20 (Admission Session: 2019-20)
Course Code: AIS 1101. Course Title: Introduction to Business
Time: 3 Hours. Full Marks: 60

Answer any Four Questions.

1. a) What do you mean by free market capitalism? What are the foundations of free market capitalism? Explain. [5]
   b) How social and economic environment are affecting business activities? Explain with examples. [5]
   c) Differentiate between franchising agreement and licensing agreement? What are the pros and cons of franchising agreement? [5]

2. a) Why are firms conducting international business? Distinguish between comparative advantage and absolute advantage with examples. [5]
   b) What is business cycle? Critically analyze the four phases of long-term business cycle. [5]
   c) Differentiate between fiscal policy and monetary policy. How does government manage the economy using fiscal policy? [5]

3. a) What are the different types of consumer products? What kinds of products would call for each of the different distribution strategies: intensive, selective and exclusive? [5]
   b) Define market targeting. What are the different market targeting strategies with examples? [5]
   c) What is marketing mix? Briefly explain the elements of marketing mix. [5]

4. a) Define management? What roles and skills are necessary for individuals in each levels of management? [5]
   b) What is SWOT analysis? Briefly explain the types of planning organizations mostly require while operating in competitive market? [5]
   c) What is business finance? Explain the types of business finance with examples. [5]

5. a) Why is the sole proprietorship the most popular form of business ownership? [5]
   b) How would you decide whether to team up with someone in a partnership form of business? [5]
   c) Are corporation merged directly by the owners? Explain. [5]

6. Write short notes on any five from the following: [15]
   a) NAFTA
   b) WTO
   c) E-Commerce
   d) SEC
   e) BSCIC
   f) Monetary policy
   g) Off-shore Banking

-The End-
