# Corporate Laws and Governance — Final Examination (2018-19)

<!-- page 1 -->
## Section A

> (Answer any two of the following questions)

## Question 1 — [Marks = 3+4+4+4=15]
**a)** Explain in brief various types of business organizations.
**b)** Distinguish between Memorandum of Association and Articles of Association.
**c)** Explain in brief the contents of a prospectus.
**d)** Explain various components of equity of a public limited company.

## Question 2 — [Marks = 4+3+3+3+2=15]
**a)** Write short note on minimum subscription and qualification shares.
**b)** Distinguish between shares and stocks.
**c)** How can a company alter its share capital?
**d)** Discuss the provisions regarding the appointment of director in public limited company.
**e)** When shall a director be incapable for the appointment as director of a company?

## Question 3 — [Marks = 3+3+3+2+4=15]
**a)** What are the contents of books of accounts according to section 181?
**b)** Describe the steps regarding the appointment of new auditor.
**c)** What are the qualifications for being the auditor of a company?
**d)** State the circumstances under which a company may be wound up by the court.
**e)** Briefly discuss the dissolution of company and committee of inspection.

## Section B

> (Answer any two of the following questions)

## Question 4 — [Marks = 3+4+4+4=15]
**a)** What is the difference between management and corporate governance?
**b)** How should company be financed: retained earnings or debt or equity or how?
**c)** Give 10 provisions of the companies act 1994 regarding corporate governance.
**d)** Who practically decide directors' remuneration of a PLC? What problem might arise in this process?

## Question 5 — [Marks = 4+4+3+4=15]
**a)** Employee profit-sharing, employee share-ownership and employee share option together work as an internal control system. Explain.
**b)** Give the name of five hidden costs of control
**c)** Explain the role of institutional shareholders in corporate governance.
**d)** Stakeholders have to be cautious about their companies' CSR activities. Why?

## Question 6 — [Marks = 4+3+4+4=15]
**a)** Nonexecutive directors are needed to curb agency costs but again they cannot help much. Do you agree? Explain.
**b)** Distinguish between the corporate governance regulations of Bangladesh Securities and Exchange Commission (BSEC) and Bangladesh Bank.
**c)** BSEC corporate governance code requires every company must have nonexecutive directors in Board but the BSEC itself does not have any nonexecutive member un its Board (SEC of other countries have nonexecutives). Comment on this.
**d)** Market is a strong corporate governance mechanism but it has imperfections and therefore, we need corporate governance. Do you agree? Explain.
