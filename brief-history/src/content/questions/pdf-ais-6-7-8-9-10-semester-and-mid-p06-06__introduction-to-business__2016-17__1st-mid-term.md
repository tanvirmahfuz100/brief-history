# Introduction to Business — 1st Midterm Exam (2016-17)

> Answer all the questions

<!-- page 6 -->
## Question 1 — [3]
What indicates that people in the United States have a high standard of living? What are some signs that maintaining such a high standard of living may have a negative impact on quality of life? Does everyone in the United States enjoy a high standard of living? If not, how does this impact their quality of life? [3]

## Question 2 — [3]
Show your understanding of the principles of supply and demand by looking at the oil market today. Why does the price of oil fluctuate so greatly? What will happen as more and more people in China and India decide to buy automobiles? What would happen if most U.S consumers decided to drive electric cars? [3]

## Question 3 — [4]
Suppose Representative I. M. Wright delivers a passionate speech at your college on tariffs. He argues tariffs are needed to

**a)** Protect our young industries.

**b)** Encourage consumers to buy U.S.-made products because it’s patriotic.

**c)** Protect U.S. jobs and wages.

**d)** Achieve a favorable balance of trade and balance of payments.

Do you agree with Representative Wright? Evaluate each of his major points and decide whether you consider it valid. Be sure to justify your position. [4]
