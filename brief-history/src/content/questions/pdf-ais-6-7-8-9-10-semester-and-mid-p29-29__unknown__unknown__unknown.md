Mr. Rahman opens a transportation consulting business called The High Flyers and completes the following transactions

| Date | Transactions |
|------|-------------|
| April 1 | Mr. Rahman invested Tk.75,000 cash along with Tk.22,000 of office equipment. |
| April 2 | Prepaid Tk.13,000 cash for two months' rent for an office. (Hint: Debit Prepaid Rent for Tk.18,000) |
| April 5 | Made credit purchases of office equipment for Tk.38,000 and office supplies for Tk.22,000. |
| April 6 | Completed a Tk.55,000 projects for a client, who will pay within 10 days. |
| April 7 | Completed services for a client and immediately received Tk.27,000 cash. |
| April 12 | Paid cash to settle the account payable created on April 5. |
| April 19 | Paid Tk.32,000 cash for the premium on a 6-month insurance policy. |
| April 22 | Received payment for the work completed on April 6. |
| April 25 | Completed work for another client for Tk.27,500 on credit. |
| April 27 | Mr. Rahman withdrew Tk.2,000 cash from the business for personal use. |
| April 29 | Purchased Tk.9,000 of additional office supplies on credit. |
| April 30 | Paid Tk.24,000 cash for this month's utility bill. |

Required
(a) Prepare general journal entries to record these transactions (use the account titles listed in part 2).
(b) Open the following accounts (use the balance column format): Cash (101); Accounts Receivable (104); Office Supplies (125); Prepaid Insurance (126); Prepaid Rent (130); Office Equipment (160); Accounts Payable (200); Mr. Rahman, Capital (302); Mr. Rahman, Withdrawals (303); Services Revenue (404); and Utilities Expense (790). Post journal entries to the accounts and enter the balance after each posting.
(c) Prepare a trial balance as of the end of this month's operations.
