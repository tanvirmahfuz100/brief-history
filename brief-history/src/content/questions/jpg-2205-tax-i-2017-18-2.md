5. **a)** Mr. A purchased a machine at a total cost of Tk. 500,000 on 23rd April 2013 for his profession, paid Tk. 10,000 legal fees, and spent Tk. 50,000 for improvement. On 30th December 2016 he sold it for Tk. 600,000 when accumulated depreciation was Tk. 126,000; fair market value was Tk. 650,000. He incurred advertisement cost Tk. 20,000 and 1.5% brokerage commission. He has taxable income Tk. 230,000 from other sources. Compute capital gains, total taxable income and tax thereon for assessment year 2017-18. [05]
   **b)** Mr. H purchased land for Tk. 10,000 on 1 January 2011 within Chittagong Municipality, paid Tk. 50,000 finders’ fee and Tk. 120,000 registration fee, and sold it on 15 July 2016 for Tk. 22,000,000, paying 2% brokerage. Fair market value on 30 June 2017 was Tk. 20,000,000. He purchased another land for Tk. 10,000,000 or Tk. 12,000,000 and expressed intent to roll over capital gain. He also had taxable income of Tk. 200,000 from interest and Tk. 100,000 from other sources. Compute capital gain and tax liability for assessment year 2017-18. [06]
   **c)** Mr. W purchased 50,000 shares of EXIM Bank at Tk. 475 per share, received a cash dividend of Tk. 10 per share and a 1-for-4 stock dividend, then sold 15,000 shares in December 2017 at Tk. 800 per share, including bonus shares. Brokerage on sale was 1%. Compute capital gain. [04]
