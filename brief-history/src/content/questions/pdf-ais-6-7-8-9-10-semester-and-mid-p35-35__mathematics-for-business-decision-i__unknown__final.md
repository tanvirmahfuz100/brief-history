# Mathematics for Business Decision I — Final Examination
<!-- page 35 -->
1. **a)** What is the difference between subset and proper subset?
   **b)** If $A=\{1,2,3,4,5\}$, verify that $A\cap(B\cup C)=(A\cap B)\cup(A\cap C)$.
   **c)** A survey of professional practice found that 185 practiced industry, 120 practiced accounting, and 160 practiced both practice and audit; other category counts are [illegible]. Indicate how many did not practice any of these.
   **d)** In a survey conducted of 5000 clerks, 28% preferred coffee, 35% liked tea, and 28% used $C$ and $T$ [illegible]. Find the number having all three preferences.
2. **a)** Define integers, prime numbers, real numbers and imaginary numbers with examples.
   **b)** If $\frac{m}{n}=\frac{m+n}{m-n}$, prove that $m=1+n$ [illegible].
   **c)** Simplify the given algebraic expression. [illegible]
   **d)** If $x=2\frac{1}{4}+3\frac{1}{4}=3\frac{1}{4}x-3\frac{1}{4}$, show that $3(x^2+y^2)^2=64$. [illegible]
   **e)** If $\log\frac{x+y}{x-y}=\frac12(\log x+\log y)$, prove $\frac{x}{y}+\frac{y}{x}=17$.
   **f)** If $\frac{\log x}{\log y}=\frac{\log y}{\log z}=\frac{\log z}{\log x}$ and $x^3y^2z=1$, prove $k=-8$.
   **g)** Prove that $7\log\frac{10}{25}+10\log\frac{25}{24}+13\log\frac{25}{10}+\log\frac{4}{5}=0$.
   **h)** Show that the logarithmic expression [illegible] equals $\frac{2}{2}$.
3. **a)** Solve the equation $\frac{x}{x-?}+\frac{?}{x-?}=\frac{?}{x-?}$.
   **b)** If 12 articles were sold for Tk. 3,000 making a profit of 25% on the first and 10% on the rest, selling them for Tk. 3,070, the profit realized would have been 15% on the first and [illegible] on the rest. Find the cost price of each.
   **c)** A commodity is produced using 3 units of labour and 2 units of capital. Total cost is 62. If produced using 4 units of labour and 1 unit of capital, cost is 56. What is the cost per unit of labour and capital?
   **d)** In perfect competition, demand is $P=20-3P-P^2$ and supply is $S=5P$. Find equilibrium price and quantity.
4. **a)** Differentiate between cooperation and combination.
   **b)** In how many ways can the letters of “ABRACADABRA” be arranged? How many have the two As together; the two Rs together; and the two As and the two Rs together?
   **c)** The sum of an arithmetic progression is 60 and the arithmetic mean of first and last terms is 18. Show that the 4 terms are 18, 16, 14 and 12. [illegible]
   **d)** Find the number of combinations formed if the letters of “Examination” are taken four at a time when (i) all four letters are different, (ii) two are different and two alike, (iii) two pairs of alike letters.
5. **a)** What is annuity? Differentiate between ordinary annuity and annuity due.
   **b)** Suppose Tk. 1,000,000 are invested for 10 years at 8% interest compounded continuously. What is the effective rate of interest? Determine the value of the original principal after 10 years.
   **c)** A machine costs Tk. 970,000 and effective life is estimated at 15 years. If scrap value is Tk. 20,000, what amount should be retained out of profit at the end of each year for replacement at compound interest of 8% per annum?
