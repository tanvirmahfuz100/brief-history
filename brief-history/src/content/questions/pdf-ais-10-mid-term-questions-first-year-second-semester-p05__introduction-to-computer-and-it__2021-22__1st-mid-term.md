## Question 1 — [10]

**a)** What is computer? How are analog computers different from digital computers?

**b)** What is the difference between data and information? Differentiate between hardware and software.

**c)** What does an image scanner do? State the advantages of digital cameras over normal cameras.

**d)** What are the advantages and disadvantages of CRT monitors and LCD monitors.

**e)** Define plotter. What is the difference between an impact printer and a nonimpact printer?
