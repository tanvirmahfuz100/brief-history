- Medical Allowance- 10% of the Basic Pay
- Entertainment Allowance- 10% of the Basic Salary
- Employer's Contribution to Recognized Provident Fund- 10% of the Basic Salary

## Question 5 — [5+6+4]
**a.** Mr. A has purchased a machine at a total cost of tk. 500,000 on 23<sup>rd</sup> April 2013 for the purpose of his profession. In addition, he has paid tk. 10,000 as legal fees. He has spent an additional sum of tk. 50,000 for improvement of the machine. On 30<sup>th</sup> December 2016, he had sold the machine at a total price of tk. 600,000 when the balance of allowable accumulated depreciation was tk. 126,000 in the books of accounts. But the fair market value on that date amounts to tk. 650,000 in the opinion of DCT. He incurred advertisement cost of tk. 20,000 and 1.5% as brokerage commission on the sale value. He has taxable income of tk. 230,000 from other sources. Compute capital gains, total taxable income and specify tax thereon for the assessment year 2017-18. [5]
**b.** Mr. H runs a business of his own. He has purchased a piece of land at a cost of tk.-10,000 on 1 January 2011 within the jurisdiction of Chittagong Municipality having a total population of around 50,000 according to the last census. He has paid tk. 50,000 as finders' fee and another tk. 120,000 as registration fee for the land. He has sold the land on July 15, 2016 at a price of tk. 22,000,000. For the sale, he has to pay brokerage fee @ 2% on the gross sales amount receipt. In DCT's opinion, the fair market value of the land at the time of sale is tk. 20,000,000. On June 30, 2017 he has purchased another land at a cost of (a) tk. 10,000,000 (b) tk. 12,000,000 and expressed his intention of getting roll over capital gain of new land in writing to DCT. He also had tk. 200,000 as taxable income from interest on securities and another tk. 100,000 taxable income from other sources. Compute the amount of capital gain and tax liability for the assessment year 2017-18. [6]
**c.** Mr. W is a regular investor of CSE Limited. In July 2009, he has purchased 50000 shares of tk. 100 per value of EXIM Bank Ltd. @ tk. 475. Brokerage charge on that time was 1.5% on the gross value of the shares traded. In March 2011, EXIM Bank declared a cash dividend of tk. 10 per share and 1 for 4 stock dividends when the market values of such shares were tk. 600 each. In December 2017, Mr. W sold 15000 shares including all the shares that he received as bonus share in 2011@tk. 800. On the date of sale , the brokerage charge was 1% on the gross value of shares traded. Required: Compute capital gain. [4]
