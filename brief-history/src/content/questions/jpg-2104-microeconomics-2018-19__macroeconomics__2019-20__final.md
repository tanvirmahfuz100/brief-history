# Macroeconomics — Final Examination (2019-20)

<!-- page 1 -->
## Question 1 — [3+3+6+3]
**a)** How will you define macroeconomics? Differentiate microeconomics from macroeconomics.
**b)** What is the subject matter of macroeconomics? Briefly explain.
**c)** Explain circular income flows in three sector economy with Government sector.
**d)** Define national income. Distinguish between GNP and GDP.

## Question 2 — [5+5+5]
**a)** Explain different approach of calculating GDP.
**b)** What are GDP deflator and real GDP? Explain the different ways to measure GDP?
**c)** Government purchases $220 billion, Depreciation $600 billion, Consumption $600 billion, Business Investment $80 billion, Exports $200 billion, Imports $220 billion, Income receipts from the rest of the world $100 billion, Income payment to rest of the world $8 billion. Compute the following:
**(i)** What is the value of GDP
**(ii)** What is the value of net exports?
**(iii)** What is the value of NNP?

## Question 3 — [5+5+5]
**a)** What are the determinants of desired national saving? What are the reasons for shifting the saving curve?
**b)** Suppose we have a consumption function in the form $C = 100 + 0.75Y$; Find out Equilibrium Output ($Y$) for each value of $Y = 300, 500, 700, 900, 1100, 1300$ and $1500$ considering net tax ($T$), planned investment expenditure ($I$) and government purchases ($G$) 100 for the all case. Assume that $AE = C + I + G$.
**c)** What are the economic influences of the government budget?

## Question 4 — [5+5+5]
**a)** Distinguish between absolute advantage and comparative advantage.
**b)** Discuss about the transaction motive and speculative motive for demanding the money.
**c)** What are the determinants of money demand? How changes in money supply affect the interest rate?

## Question 5 — [3+4+4+4]
**a)** What is inflation? Distinguish between demand pull and cost push inflation.
**b)** How an effective monetary policy affects the growth of the economy of a nation?
**c)** What are the factors that cause the inflation in Bangladesh?
**d)** What are the monetary measures that Bangladesh Bank should take to control inflation?

## Question 6 — [5+5+5]
**a)** Define trade barriers. What are the most common types of trade barriers?
**b)** Why public finance is important for the economic development of a country?
**c)** What are the ways of protectionism? Explain the functions NAFTA, SAFTA, EU.
