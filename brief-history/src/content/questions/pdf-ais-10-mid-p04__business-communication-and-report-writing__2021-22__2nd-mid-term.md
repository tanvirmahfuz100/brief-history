# Business Communication and Report Writing — 2nd Mid-term Examination (2021-22)

<!-- page 4 -->
> Answer all the questions. Start answering each question from a fresh sheet. Answers should be properly structured, relevant, and neat.

**Time:** 1 hour     **Marks:** 10

## Question 1
**(a).** Compare and contrast written communication with oral communication.
**(b).** Describe the business communication process.
**(c).** Effective communication in business is of importance for the proper functioning of business. Explain.
**(d).** Communication is a two-way process. Explain.

## Question 2
**(a).** What do you think can be the major objectives of business communication? Explain any two of them.
**(b).** Explain the significance of written communication.
**(c).** Mention the benefits of face-to-face communication.
**(d).** In corporate environment, gossiping may not always be bad! Do you agree? Justify your position.
