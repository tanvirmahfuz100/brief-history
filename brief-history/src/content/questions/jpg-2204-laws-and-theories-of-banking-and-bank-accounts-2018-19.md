# Laws and Theories of Banking and Bank Accounts — Final (2018-19)

<!-- page 1 -->
> Answer any four of the following questions

**Time:** 3.00 Hours     **Full Marks:** 60

## Question 1 — [5+5+5=15]
**(a)** What do you mean by Bank and Bank Management? Distinguish between bank and banking.
**(b)** What are the internal and external factors that influence in the determination of the level of deposit?
**(c)** Briefly describe the role of bank in the economic development of Bangladesh.

## Question 2 — [5+5+5=15]
**(a)** What do you mean by the term 'liquidity'? Do you think that liquidity and profitability means same? Present your opinion.
**(b)** What are the different reasons underlie behind the liquidity crisis arising in Bank?
**(c)** Explain the liquidity management strategies adopted by the banks to deal with liquidity problem.

## Question 3 — [5+5+5=15]
**(a)** Narrate the objective criteria of loan classification.
**(b)** Is there any relationship between non-performing loan and economic development in Bangladesh? Discuss.
**(c)** What is non-performing loan? Do you believe weak governance mechanism increase the non-performing loan in Bangladesh? Discuss.

## Question 4 — [5+5+5=15]
**(a)** Briefly describe the green banking activities performed by commercial banks in Bangladesh.
**(b)** Define camel rating. Explain the composite score of '5' and '2' of camel rating.
**(c)** Discuss the initiatives have taken by the international community for reducing money laundering.

## Question 5 — [5+5+5=15]
**(a)** Define money laundering according to 'money Laundering Prevention Act 2012'. Discuss the relationship between moral values and money laundering.
**(b)** What are the penalties of money laundering? What are the consequences of money laundering for the economic development of Bangladesh?
**(c)** Briefly discuss about the penalties of terrorist financing. Do you believe casino business increase money laundering in Bangladesh? Justify your answer in light with casino business in Bangladesh.

Write short notes on any five:
**(a)** Loan classification
**(b)** Fiscal policy and monetary policy
**(c)** Scoring model
**(d)** Money Laundering Prevention Act 2012
**(e)** SWIFT
**(f)** CAMEL rating framework
**(g)** Green banking
