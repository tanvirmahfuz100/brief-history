**Course Code: AIS 1103. Course Name: Business Communication and Report Writing**

> [Answer any FOUR of the following questions. Figures in the right margin indicate full marks. Examiner will take account of the quality of language and of the manner in which the answers are presented. Different parts, if any, of the same question must be answered in order of the given sequence.]

## Question 1 — [5+5+5=15]
**a)** What is the significance of effective business communication in today's corporate world?

**b)** Can you explain the key differences between business communication and casual communication?

**c)** Imagine you're a manager introducing a new product to your team. How would you effectively communicate the features and benefits of the product to ensure everyone understands and is motivated?


## Question 2 — [7+4+4=15]
**a)** What changes has computer technology brought about in the field of communication? What are the advantages of this technology? What are its limitations?

**b)** "One should feed, water and cultivate the grapevine rather than try to curb its growth." Discuss.

**c)** How does the grapevine operate in an organization? How can its harmful effects be controlled?


## Question 3 — [5+3+7=15]
**a)** What are the essentials of good business letter? Discuss, with examples, the importance of accuracy, completeness and clarity in a business letter.

**b)** A good business letter never says 'No'. Do you agree?

**c)** Rewrite the following sentences to eliminate negative aspects.

**i.** I regret to say that I cannot send you the book you order, for it is under print.

**ii.** It is not possible for us to mention the exact price of the sofa-set you want for you have failed to give any specifications

**iii.** The raw materials being in short supply, we cannot dispatch the goods until June 10.

**iv.** Unfortunately you have failed to attach the project report, because of which we cannot consider your loan application.

**v.** You will never regret that you decided to join a bank.


## Question 4 — [6+3+6=15]
**a)** What do you mean by solicited and unsolicited enquiries? Make an unsolicited offer to a departmental store undertaking to offer a regular supply of a variety of children's education toys.

**b)** "A customer's claim or complaint opens the way for an investigation. It also becomes an opportunity to right a wrong and to promote goodwill." Explain and illustrate.

**c)** Draft a suitable reply to a customer who has complained about the poor service of the laptop supplied by you.


## Question 5 — [3+6+6=15]
**a)** To whom should a memo report never be sent? Why not?

**b)** Draft a letter to Guisan Traders, Wuhan, China, thanking them for their order for some handicraft items and asking them for detailed dispatch instructions and an irrevocable confirmed letter of credit.

**c)** Write a letter to one of the shareholders explaining why your Company is offering rights shares of TK. 10 at a premium of Tk. 50.


## Question 6 — [5+5+5=15]
**a)** What steps should a job candidate take to prepare for a job interview effectively?

**b)** Explain the differences between APA, MLA, and Chicago citation styles in academic writing.

**c)** Write a short research paper on a topic of your choice and apply proper citation and referencing using a specific academic style (e.g., APA, MLA, Chicago). Explain the importance of accurate referencing.
