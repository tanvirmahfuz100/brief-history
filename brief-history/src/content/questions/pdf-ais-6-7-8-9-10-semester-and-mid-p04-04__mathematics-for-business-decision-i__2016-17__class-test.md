# Mathematics for Business Decision-1 — First Class Test (2016-2017)

<!-- page 4 -->

1. Define integers, prime numbers, real numbers and imaginary number with example. [1]

2. In a survey conducted of 5000 clerks in an office. It was found that 48% preferred coffee (C), 54% liked tea (T) and 64% used to smoke (S) of the total 28% used C and T, 32% used T and S and 30% preferred C and S. only 6% did none of these. Find (i) the number having all the three (ii) T and S but not C (iii) only C. [3]

3. If $\dfrac{9^{3-2n}\,(27)^{n-1}}{3^{3n}} = \dfrac{1}{27}$, prove that $m = 1+n$. [2]

4. If $x = 3^{1/4} + 5^{-1/4}$, $y = 3^{1/4} - 3^{-1/4}$ show that $3(x^2+y^2)^2 = \ldots$ [3] [illegible]

5. If $A = \{1,2,3,4,5,6\}$, $B = \{2,9,6\}$, $C = \{1,2,5\}$ and $U = \{1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8\}$ Compute the following: (i) $A'$ (ii) $B' \cup C'$ (iii) $(A \cap C)$ (iv) $A \cup C$ [3] [partly illegible]
